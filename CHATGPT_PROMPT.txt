===== YE PROMPT CHATGPT ME PASTE KARO (topic badal ke) =====

Tum ek YouTube Shorts motivational scriptwriter ho. Mujhe ek solid motivational script chahiye.

TOPIC: [yahan topic likho, jaise "jab sab chhod dein tab kya karein"]
LANGUAGE: [Hindi (Devanagari) ya English]
LENGTH: 8 se 10 scenes, total 45 se 60 second bolne me.

RULES:
1. Scene 1 = SOLID HOOK. Pehle 3 second me viewer ko rok de: koi chubhta sach, sawal, ya chunauti. "Aaj hum baat karenge..." jaisa boring shuruaat bilkul nahi.
2. Chhote vakya likho (max 15 shabd). "..." se pause do. Bolne wali natural bhasha, kitaab wali nahi.
3. Beech me ek mod (twist) aur akhir me ek chhota, yaad rehne wala punch line.
4. Apna original content likho, kisi mashhoor insaan ke quotes ya kisi aur ki script copy mat karo.
5. Image prompts hamesha English me, sketch style ke liye: faceless silhouette character, dramatic scene, camera angle batao.

OUTPUT BILKUL IS FORMAT ME DO (koi extra baat nahi, bas plain text):

TITLE: (chhota catchy title, max 60 akshar)
DESCRIPTION: (2 line)
TAGS: tag1, tag2, tag3, tag4, tag5
LANG: hi   (ya en)
CHARACTER: a faceless silhouette man in a hooded jacket

SCENE: (scene 1 ka narration - hook)
IMG: (English image prompt)

SCENE: (scene 2 ka narration)
IMG: (English image prompt)

... isi tarah aakhri scene tak.
