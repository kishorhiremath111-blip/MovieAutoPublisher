Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

MovieAutoPublisher ("we", "our", or "the app") is an automation tool designed to help users manage and publish their own or properly licensed video content to supported platforms.

1. Information We Collect

MovieAutoPublisher may process information required to authenticate and operate connected publishing services, including account information, authentication tokens, channel/page information, video metadata, and upload status.

2. Google and YouTube Access

If you connect a Google or YouTube account, the application may request permission to upload videos and access information necessary to manage those uploads.

Google account information is used only for the functionality requested by the user. We do not sell Google user data or use it for unrelated advertising purposes.

3. Authentication Tokens

OAuth credentials and tokens are used to authenticate authorized actions. They should be stored securely and are not intentionally shared publicly. Users can revoke access through their connected Google account settings.

4. Video Content

The application may process video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and other metadata supplied by the user for the purpose of preparing and publishing content.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights, licenses, or permissions for content they upload or publish.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when required to provide functionality requested by the user, such as video publishing and authentication.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect authentication credentials and other information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain upload records, or comply with applicable requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

MovieAutoPublisher is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with services such as Google, YouTube, Facebook, and Firebase. Their respective privacy policies and terms may apply to information processed by those services.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the MovieAutoPublisher developer through the developer contact information associated with the application.
